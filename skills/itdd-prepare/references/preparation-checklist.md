# Planning handoff checklist

- [ ] The request and relevant repository context were checked; unknown decisions were asked, not guessed.
- [ ] `wayfinder` or `grill-with-docs` was used when its trigger applied.
- [ ] The published spec follows `to-spec`, including its human seam check.
- [ ] The human agreed to the `to-tickets` breakdown before tickets were published.
- [ ] Published tickets link to the source and show acceptance criteria and blocking edges.
- [ ] The handoff links the actual published artifacts and identifies unresolved questions, exclusions, and the suggested next move.
- [ ] A required formal approval, if any, used `/approved` with a supported recording interface; ordinary planning decisions did not.
- [ ] No implementation, BUILD, TEST, VERIFY, promotion, or execution handoff began.
